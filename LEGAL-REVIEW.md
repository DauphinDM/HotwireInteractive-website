# Legal publication checklist

The `/privacy/`, `/terms/`, and `/cookies/` pages are working drafts matched to the current site code, not a legal compliance certification. Have the operator and a suitably qualified adviser review them before publication. Do not treat the implementation or these drafts as a substitute for legal advice.

## Details the owner must confirm before publishing

**Current owner status:** the company has not been registered yet; legal/registration details will be supplied later. Leave those details unfilled, keep the agreement as a review draft, and do not enable final agreement issuance or payment collection on that basis. No company form, enterprise/VAT number, or tax exemption should be invented. The actual individual/operator responsible for a public inquiry website may still need to be identified before registration; obtain appropriate review rather than assuming the current notices are complete.

- **Legal operator/controller identity:** full registered name (or the actual individual operating the business), legal form where applicable, service/registered address, Belgian enterprise number, and VAT number if applicable. Only the supplied studio name, country, and contact email have been used; no registration details were invented. Add the verified details to the website and privacy/terms pages and determine whether a separate company/legal notice is required.
- **Hosting and email providers:** their actual identities, processing locations, data-processing terms, subprocessors, backup/log retention, and any transfers outside the EEA and relevant safeguards. Provider categories are described publicly, but those operational facts cannot be inferred from the source code.
- **Retention schedule:** choose and implement appropriate periods for unsuccessful inquiries, client correspondence, project records, logs, backups, and any legal retention duties. The notices intentionally do not invent an automatic mailbox-deletion period.
- **Lawful bases and consent wording:** confirm the appropriate basis for each inquiry/security activity and whether the required confirmation in the form needs adjustment. It must not be repurposed as marketing consent.
- **Remote fonts:** Google Fonts currently receives direct browser requests. Confirm the appropriate privacy/transfer arrangements; self-hosting licensed font files is an alternative if remote requests are unsuitable. The privacy notice discloses those requests rather than claiming the site has no third parties.
- **Cookie/storage rules:** review essential contact-session storage and the user-requested quiz transfer under applicable Belgian/EU rules. There are no analytics/ad scripts in the source; verify the live host does not inject other tracking. If optional tracking is added, introduce any required prior consent controls before enabling it.
- **Commercial terms:** `/terms/` remains website-use terms only. `/websites/agreement/` now has a printable review template, explicitly not an issued offer, invoice, or document to sign. Complete and review separate service terms for scope, ownership/licences, changes, acceptance, payment, cancellation/refunds/withdrawal rights, support, privacy, disputes, and consumer protections before issuing an actual agreement. Do not impose blanket “non-refundable” deposits.
- **Pricing and tax:** package ranges are planning budgets. Confirm the business VAT status, customer-facing tax presentation, and gross total payable before publishing binding commercial offers. Do not assume VAT exemption or automatically add 21% without establishing the applicable treatment. The draft tool accepts a proposed tax-inclusive total but does not calculate tax.
- **Postal address:** the supplied agreement-return address is Max Hermanelei 55, Brasschaat 2930, Belgium. Confirm use and deliverability; do not infer it is a registered office.
- **Kickoff verification:** manual signature/version checks and cleared-deposit reconciliation are required before work. No gateway, online signing, or automated verification exists. The public tools must not be represented as evidence of signature or payment. See `ai_here/kickoff-checklist.md`.

## Verified against the implementation

- **Translations:** pages, fixed UI messages, and client confirmations are available in English/Dutch/French/German from local catalogues. The rough initial output was replaced with whole-message software drafts and an LLM editorial/meaning pass, with LanguageTool checking. The HTML-email follow-up brings the current totals to 981 source messages and 587 curated messages. Build checks preserve amounts, percentages, identities, and important wording. This is not native-speaker signoff, certified legal translation, or legal approval. Review commercial/legal copy appropriately. The agreement remains not for signature/payment in every language; registration/VAT details remain deferred. Maintenance services receive only checked-in public copy after explicit opt-in, never visitor fields.
- **Language preference:** only an explicit choice writes `hotwire-language` to local storage, holding a locale code until changed or cleared. Browser detection alone does not persist anything. This is disclosed in privacy/storage notices. The inquiry includes a validated communication-language preference; it does not translate the visitor's message or send private data to a translation service. Review the applicable legal treatment of preference storage rather than assuming a blanket exemption.

- Contact recipient: `contact@hotwireinteractive.co.uk`; fixed mail sender: `website@hotwireinteractive.co.uk`.
- Contact service: PHP, validated personal details/message/brief, consent confirmation, CSRF, a honeypot, rate limits, and duplicate-payload checks. No attachments accepted.
- Email confirmation: a transactional HTML/plain-text message is attempted only after studio-mail acceptance; no submitted name, message, or project brief is reflected to the unverified address. No tracking pixels, remote email fonts/images, marketing subscription, contract issuance, or payment confirmation is added. Failure/suppression does not reject an accepted inquiry.
- Session cookie: `hotwire_contact`, API path `/api/`, session lifetime, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, Secure when PHP sees HTTPS. Review HTTPS/proxy configuration on the actual host.
- Quiz transfer: `hotwire-website-brief` in session storage, ten choices, optional allowlisted design ID, and timestamp only; accepted for ten minutes and removed when read. An unread entry can physically remain until the tab's session ends or storage is cleared.
- Demo basket/booking selections and agreement draft fields stay in page memory. No POST requests, application browser storage, signature capture, or payment processing are used by these tools. Printing/PDF creates a user-controlled local copy. A selected design ID can appear in URLs and ordinary request logs.
- Receipt matching: active window of one hour; hashed-IP request counting: per-IP fifteen-minute and global one-hour windows. Pruning happens during later processing, not through a scheduled deletion job.
- Confirmation protection: keyed recipient-address hashes and attempt timestamps remain active for a rolling 24 hours; limits are one/15 minutes and three/day per recipient, 50/hour and 200/day globally. No raw addresses/messages are stored in that file. Expired entries are pruned on later checks; an idle file can remain until host cleanup. Mail-provider logs and inbox retention are separate. Review lawful basis, retention, host access, and pseudonymisation without describing hashes as anonymous data.
- Names, email addresses, and message text are not written to browser storage by the application. Email infrastructure necessarily processes submitted inquiry content.
- Public pages link from every site footer and both contact-form variants. No marketing consent checkbox, fake cookie-preference controls, or tracking banner was introduced.

After any processing, provider, or legal-identity changes, update these pages and their “Last updated” dates together. Verify the deployed forms and storage, not only the local source.
