What changes the estimate?

Content editing adds a planning allowance of €250–€500; writing from scratch adds €750–€1,500. An under-four-week request adds a 20% allowance, subject to availability. Extra languages, bespoke artwork, migration, and integrations need separate scoping. These are planning rules, not automatic charges.

Hosting, domain renewals, CMS/app licences, transaction fees, maintenance, and content after launch are not bundled indefinitely. Your quote must identify their provider, billing period, owner, and cost. Search ranking and revenue are never guaranteed.
