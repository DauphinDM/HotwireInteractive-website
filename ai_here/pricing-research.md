## What this supports

- A clear distinction between low-cost self-build software, annual done-for-you subscriptions, and a one-off scoped studio build is essential.
- Hotwire's €500 minimum is viable only as a tightly constrained supplied-content template entry offer. It cannot credibly include custom accounts, complex integrations, copywriting, or unlimited maintenance.
- Retain the proposed four ranges, but tie them to effort and independently enforced feature minimums. €500–€1,200 / €1,200–€3,000 / €3,000–€6,500 / €6,500–€14,000+ are **Hotwire planning decisions**, not competitor-validated market averages.
- Content allowances (€250–€500 partial; €750–€1,500 full) and 20% rush planning allowance are explicit internal rules, not sourced industry norms. Availability, price, and any actual surcharge require agreement.
- Enterprise has no fixed ceiling. A small app or portal can exceed €14,000; paid discovery and a separate specification may be appropriate.

## Comparison limitations

The offers differ in labour, support, platform ownership, exportability, hosting, renewals, transaction fees, licensing, and cancellation terms. Three-year arithmetic is illustrative—not a quotation, net-present-value calculation, or like-for-like savings claim. Hotwire recurring costs have not been invented and must be stated separately in each quote. Business tax status remains unconfirmed; do not assume the competitor VAT basis is Hotwire's basis. No “cheapest” claim is warranted.
