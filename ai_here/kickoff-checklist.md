# Manual kickoff gate — copy to a PRIVATE project record

This is an operational checklist, not software verification or legal advice. Never fill it with real client information in the public repository/build output. The website cannot establish postal arrival, authentic signatures, or cleared funds.

**Rule: no design/development work starts until both gates below pass and the studio sends written kickoff confirmation. An inquiry, quiz estimate, template choice, draft PDF, invoice, client checkbox, or payment screenshot never passes either gate.**

## Before issuing an agreement

- Confirm registered operator identity, service address, registration/VAT details and applicable tax presentation. Obtain appropriate commercial/consumer-law review.
- Freeze proposal reference/version, client legal identity and signing authority, chosen direction, deliverables, exclusions, revision limits, content responsibilities, schedule, acceptance/milestone criteria, ownership/licences, recurring provider costs, support, changes, cancellation/refunds, privacy, and disputes.
- Specify gross total payable, any tax breakdown, the exact 50% invoice amount and reference, milestone 25%, remaining 25%, due dates, and payment instructions. Round in cents; final instalment absorbs rounding differences.
- Issue the completed reviewed agreement yourself. The website-generated **review draft must not be signed or used as a payment demand**.

## Gate A — agreement verified

- Receive the returned issued agreement (postal return: **Max Hermanelei 55, Brasschaat 2930, Belgium**) or the separately agreed signature route.
- Compare reference/version, all pages/attachments, scope, totals, amendments, signatures, dates, and signer authority. Obtain countersignature where appropriate.
- Resolve missing pages, changes, ambiguous authority, or mismatched versions before approval.
- In the secured project record, record verifier, verification date, agreement reference/version, and a restricted evidence location.

## Gate B — cleared 50% deposit verified

- Reconcile the invoice reference/project/customer, exact required amount, and EUR currency against the studio's own bank or payment-provider records.
- Confirm actual clearance/availability under the agreed payment method—not a browser redirect, pending authorization, client receipt, or screenshot. Investigate short/overpayments and mismatched senders/references.
- Record verifier, verification date, invoice reference, reconciled amount/currency, bank/provider transaction reference, and restricted evidence location. Do not record card data or credentials.
- Hold kickoff for pending, failed, reversed, refunded, disputed, unmatched, or insufficient payments until resolved under the reviewed agreement.

## Release

- An authorised studio person checks that **A AND B** refer to the same current agreement and project.
- Send written kickoff confirmation naming the accepted scope/reference, cleared deposit, agreed start/target dates, client dependencies, and next milestone.
- Record authoriser/time privately. Schedule the work only now.
- Material scope changes need written approval and updated financial milestones. Refunds/cancellations/reversals must reopen the review where appropriate; never silently keep an old approval.

## If later automated

Use a private authenticated server-side project system, signed/idempotent gateway webhooks, verified signature-provider evidence, exact amount/currency checks, audit history, least-privilege roles, retention/access controls, and reconciliation. Client-supplied state must never mark a deposit “verified”. No such system is implemented here.
