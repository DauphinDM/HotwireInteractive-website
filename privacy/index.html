Your privacy.

What happens to your information when you browse, take the quiz, or contact the studio.

Who we are

Hotwire Interactive is an independent studio based in Belgium and operates this website. For questions about this notice or your information, email contact@hotwireinteractive.co.uk.

This notice covers this website and initial inquiries. If you become a client, a project may require additional agreements about personal data.

Information we handle

  • Contact inquiries: your name or studio, email address, inquiry type, message, any attached quiz/design brief, and confirmation that we may use those details to respond.
  • Quiz answers: the quiz runs in your browser. Its ten answers are not sent to the studio just because you complete it. Choosing “Discuss this website” carries the choices and any selected design ID to the contact page in the same tab. The brief reaches the server only if you send the inquiry.
  • Design selection: a fixed design ID can appear in the page URL and ordinary request logs. Choosing a design prepares an inquiry; it does not submit one automatically.
  • Local demonstrations and draft: demo basket/booking selections and agreement-review fields remain in the current page’s memory. These tools do not submit or write their contents to application browser storage. Printing or saving PDF creates a copy under your control.
  • Security information: the contact service uses a session identifier, security tokens, short-lived submission receipts, and hashed IP-based request counts to prevent forgery, duplicate submissions, and abuse.
  • Technical requests: hosting and email infrastructure may process IP addresses, browser information, request times, and delivery or security logs when providing the service.

Please do not submit passwords, payment details, health information, or other sensitive personal data. Attachments are not accepted by the contact form.

Why we use it

We use inquiry information to understand your request, respond, and take steps towards a possible project at your request. These pre-contractual steps can be a legal basis under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For general correspondence, reliable operation, and prevention of abuse, we rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f), balanced against your rights.

The form asks you to confirm that we may use your information to respond. This is not a marketing subscription. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us; withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before it was withdrawn.

You do not need to submit contact details to browse or receive a quiz result. Without the required inquiry information, we cannot handle a form submission. The quiz is a rule-based suggestion, not a decision with legal or similarly significant effects.

Providers & transfers

Our hosting and email providers process information needed to operate the website, accept your inquiry, deliver email, and maintain security. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to address misuse. Inquiry information is not used to build advertising audiences.

Google Fonts: this site currently loads Manrope and Space Grotesk from Google’s font services. Your browser connects to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which makes network information such as your IP address and browser request details available to Google. See Google Fonts privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Processing locations depend on the provider. Google’s services may involve processing outside the European Economic Area. Contact us for information about the providers, processing locations, and applicable transfer safeguards for your inquiry.

Links to other websites are governed by those websites’ own notices. Opening a direct email link uses your chosen email application and provider.

How long we keep it

  • Inquiry emails: for as long as needed to handle the inquiry, any resulting project, and relevant record-keeping or dispute requirements. Contact us to ask about retention or deletion of a particular conversation.
  • Quiz transfer: accepted for up to ten minutes and removed when the contact page reads it. An unused browser-storage entry may remain until the tab’s session ends or you clear it; expired entries are not used.
  • Security records: submission receipts are used for up to one hour. Request-count records cover a rolling hour and stale entries are pruned when the limiter next processes an inquiry. PHP session expiry and hosting/email log retention also depend on hosting configuration.

The site code does not store your name, email address, or message in browser storage. See Cookies & Storage for details about the essential session cookie and quiz transfer.

Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can also withdraw consent where it is the basis for processing. We may need proportionate information to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

Email contact@hotwireinteractive.co.uk with your request. We normally respond within one month; if a lawful extension is needed, we will explain it.

You may complain to your local supervisory authority. In Belgium, this is the Data Protection Authority. Contacting us first is welcome but is not a condition for making a complaint.

We may update this notice when the website or our practices change. The date above identifies the current version.
